Common Site Problems

  • Critical Worker Shortage

    Lack of available skilled manpower delays deadlines and increases site overhead costs.

  • Unannounced Absences

    Last-minute absenteeism halts work progress and strains existing site teams.

  • Difficult Skill Verification

    Hiring unverified workers leads to rework, safety hazards, and poor build quality.
